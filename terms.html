Listing on ardena is a commitment to renters, to safety, and to Ardena. These obligations run for the whole period your vehicle is listed.

  • Valid insurance. Maintain valid motor insurance throughout the listing period that covers third-party risks at minimum (per the Insurance (Motor Vehicles Third Party Risks) Act, Cap. 405). It is your responsibility to confirm with your insurer that the policy permits rental use; many private-use policies exclude it.
  • Roadworthy and inspected. Keep the vehicle roadworthy and maintain a current NTSA-recognised inspection certificate where applicable.
  • Honest listing. Photos must be current, clear, and unaltered. Descriptions must accurately state condition, mileage, and any limitations. Misrepresentation is a breach of these Terms.
  • Availability and handover. Make the vehicle available at the agreed time and place, or give the renter reasonable advance notice and a reasonable alternative if circumstances change.
  • Don't cancel without good cause. Repeated or last-minute cancellations may attract penalties deducted from future payouts, and may lead to listing suspension.
  • Clean and empty. Hand over the vehicle clean and free of your personal belongings.
  • Respond and report. Respond to booking requests and renter messages promptly through the app, and report any incident, damage, or theft immediately.
  • No employment relationship. Listing on ardena does not make you an employee, agent, or partner of Ardena. We do not guarantee bookings, occupancy, or revenue.
  • Tax. You are responsible for your own tax obligations, including any income from the Platform. Provide a valid KRA PIN for payouts and tax withholding where applicable.
  • Valid NTSA-recognised inspection certificate dated within the last 12 months (where required for the vehicle class).
  • Valid comprehensive or third-party motor insurance policy, confirmed by the host as covering rental use.
  • No outstanding finance, court order, or police flag restricting transfer or use of the vehicle.
  • Functional seatbelts for every seated position, functional tyres including a serviceable spare, working headlights and indicators, and a first-aid kit.
  • Current, unedited photos that accurately represent the vehicle's condition (exterior, interior, any cosmetic damage).
  • Up-to-date logbook and proof of ownership matching the host's verified identity.
  • Right of removal. Ardena may remove any listing that does not meet these standards, has repeated damage complaints, has been linked to safety incidents, or where the host fails to renew documentation on request.

07 / Bookings & payments

How bookings, payments, and payouts work.

Bookings are made and paid for in the app. Off-platform arrangements are not protected by our dispute process.

  • When a booking is confirmed. A booking is confirmed only when the host accepts and the renter receives the in-app confirmation. A request or a listing alone is not a confirmed reservation.
  • Pricing. Hosts set the daily or hourly rate. Ardena adds a platform service fee shown in the booking summary. All prices are in Kenyan Shillings (KES). Additional charges may apply for mileage overages, late returns, damage, cleaning, fines, or tolls.
  • Payment methods. M-Pesa and licensed card processors only. Cash and other off-platform payments are not recognised by Ardena and are not covered by our dispute resolution process.
  • Host payouts. Ardena disburses host earnings, net of the platform service fee and any deductions, to the host's registered M-Pesa or bank account within the period stated in the Host app. Ardena may withhold or set off payouts against amounts owed.
  • Service-fee changes. Ardena may update its service-fee rate. Material changes will be communicated to hosts at least 14 days in advance.
  • We are not a financial institution. Payment processing is performed by licensed third-party providers. Ardena is not liable for delays caused by your mobile network operator, bank, or the payment provider.
  • Disputed charges. Both sides must raise any payment dispute in the app within 48 hours of the trip ending. Ardena's decision following a documented review is final, subject only to any right of legal appeal.
  • Deposit (if any)

    A refundable deposit may be required for certain vehicles, shown at booking and held by Ardena. Refunded within 5-7 business days after trip completion provided no damage, outstanding fines, fuel deficit, or policy violation is established.

  • Renter cancellation

    More than 48 hours before pickup: full refund of the rental amount (platform booking fee is non-refundable). Within 48 hours: 50% forfeit. Within 6 hours or no-show: full forfeit.

  • Host cancellation

    Renter receives a full refund. Without valid reason, the host is liable to a penalty deducted from future payouts; repeated cancellations may lead to listing suspension.

  • Force majeure

    Natural disaster, government-declared curfew, civil unrest, or other extraordinary event beyond the parties' reasonable control: Ardena may issue full or partial refunds at its discretion.

  • Refund timing

    Refunds go back to the original payment method and may take 3-10 business days depending on the payment provider.
