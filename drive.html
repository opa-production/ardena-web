Drive with Ardena. Earn on your own terms.

Set your rates, choose your hours and take the trips you want. You keep 90% of every fare and withdraw to M-Pesa.

Apply to drive How it works

No account yet? You create one as you apply.

Why drivers choose Ardena

01 / Your rates

Set a daily rate and an hourly rate, and change them whenever you like. New rates apply to new trips, never to ones already booked.

02 / Your hours

Pick your working days and hours, go online when you're ready, and accept or decline every request. Day hires, hourly trips, or both.

03 / Paid to M-Pesa

Ardena keeps 10% of each fare. Earnings from trips paid in the app can be withdrawn to M-Pesa 24 hours after the trip. Cash trips are paid to you on the spot.

04 / Trips near you

Renters find you by town or by distance, and see your rating, languages and the cars you drive. Your exact location is never shown.

Apply in four steps

  1. 01

    Create your account

    It's the first step of the form: your name and email, or continue with Google. Already on Ardena? Just log in.

  2. 02

    Tell us how you drive

    Your experience, languages, the towns you serve, the cars you can drive, your working hours and your rates.

  3. 03

    Add your documents

    A profile photo, your driving licence and your national ID. A certificate of good conduct is optional and earns a background-checked badge.

  4. 04

    Get approved and go online

    Our team reviews every application. Once approved, open the Ardena app, switch to Chauffeur Mode and start taking trips.

Start your application

What you need

Have these ready and the application takes about ten minutes. Photos taken on your phone are fine, as long as every detail can be read.

  • A valid driving licence

    Its number and a clear photo. If your Ardena profile already has a verified licence, you won't need to upload it again.

  • Your national ID

    A photo of the front, so we can confirm who you are. Licence and ID photos are stored privately and only seen by our review team.

  • An M-Pesa number

    Where your earnings go. You'll also need a smartphone with the Ardena app to take trips once you're approved.

  • Good conduct certificate (optional)

    Recommended. Drivers with a checked certificate carry a background-checked badge that renters can see.

Questions drivers ask

Do I need an Ardena account to apply?

No. You create your account as the first step of the application, with your email or Google. If you already have one, log in and carry on.

How much can I earn?

You set your own daily and hourly rates, from KSh 500 to KSh 50,000 a day and KSh 100 to KSh 10,000 an hour. Ardena keeps 10% of each fare and you keep the rest.

Do I need my own car?

No. Most trips are in the renter's own car or a car they rented on Ardena. If you can also bring your own vehicle, say so when you apply.

How do I get paid?

For trips paid in the app, your share lands in your balance and can be withdrawn to M-Pesa 24 hours after the trip, from KSh 100. For cash trips the renter pays you directly, and Ardena's 10% comes off your next withdrawal.

Can I choose when I work?

Yes. You pick your working days and hours, switch online or offline whenever you like, and accept or decline every trip request.

What happens if a renter cancels?

Renters can cancel for free up to 24 hours before the trip. After that they get half back, and you receive your share of the rest.

Is my location shared?

Renters only ever see your area, never your exact position: to about 1 km while you are online, and about 2 km for the base you pin when you apply.

What if my application needs changes?

We tell you exactly what to fix, for example a blurry licence photo, and you resubmit just that part. Track your application anytime on your driver page.
