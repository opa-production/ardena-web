How it works

From application to payout

This is different from Refer & earn, which every ardena user gets in the app. The affiliate programme is for creators and marketers we approve one by one.

01

Apply below

Tell us where you post, your handle and how many people follow you. It takes a minute.

02

We review your profile

Our team looks at your content and audience, then calls or messages you on the number you gave us.

03

Get your own code

Once approved, you get a personal affiliate code to put in your posts, bio and videos.

04

Earn on every rental

When your followers rent a car using your code, you earn. We share the reward rates and terms with you on approval.

The fine print

Good to know

A few details before you apply.

Approval comes first

Applying doesn't make you an affiliate. You only earn once we approve you and give you your code.

Your code is yours

Each affiliate gets a personal code, so every rental it brings in is credited to you.

Keep it honest

Tell your audience it's a partnership, and don't use your own code on your own bookings. Breaking this ends the partnership.
