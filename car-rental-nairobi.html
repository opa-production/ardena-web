Car rental in Nairobi

Self drive car hire in Nairobi County with ardena: verified vehicles from local hosts, pickup near JKIA and Wilson Airport, and transparent pricing.

Car hire in Nairobi, booked online

Nairobi is where most journeys in Kenya begin. It is the country’s commercial centre, the arrival point for almost every international visitor, and the starting line for road trips to the Rift Valley, the coast, and the parks. ardena lists verified vehicles from local hosts across the capital so you can arrange car hire in Nairobi entirely online, with the price agreed before you book and no negotiation at handover.

Browse what is available for your dates in the ardena app, compare vehicles and rates side by side, and pay with M-Pesa, Visa, or Mastercard. Your host confirms pickup and return directly with you.

Where you can pick up a car in Nairobi

Hosts list from across Nairobi County rather than a single downtown depot, which usually means a pickup point closer to you than a traditional rental branch. Availability is common in and around:

  • Westlands, Parklands and Kileleshwa: convenient for business travel and short-stay apartments
  • Kilimani, Lavington and Upper Hill: close to offices, embassies, and hotels
  • Karen, Runda and Gigiri: useful for longer stays and family trips
  • Embakasi and the JKIA corridor: for early departures and late arrivals
  • The CBD, Ngong Road and Thika Road: central collection along the main routes out of the city

Enter your pickup area and dates in the app to see what is genuinely available near you.

Airport car hire: JKIA and Wilson

Flying into Jomo Kenyatta International Airport and wanting a car waiting is one of the most common reasons people search for car hire near me in Nairobi. Many ardena hosts arrange handover at or near JKIA, and the same applies at Wilson Airport for domestic and safari connections.

Because pickup is coordinated directly with your host, it is worth booking a day or two ahead for a flight arrival and sharing your landing time so the handover matches your schedule. For very early departures, agreeing the return point the evening before saves a stressful morning.

Self drive, long-term, and family vehicles

Self drive car hire suits most trips within Nairobi: you keep the keys, set your own schedule, and are not paying for a driver’s waiting time in traffic. For visitors who would rather not drive the city themselves, some hosts can arrange a driver, so ask before you book.

If you need a vehicle for several weeks, monthly car hire is often better value than repeated short bookings, and hosts on ardena frequently offer better rates for longer commitments. For weekend trips toward Naivasha, Nakuru, Amboseli, or the Mara, a higher-clearance SUV or 4x4 is the sensible choice; for meetings and airport runs inside the city, a compact saloon is cheaper to run and far easier to park.

Driving in Nairobi: what to expect

Kenya drives on the left. Nairobi traffic is heaviest roughly 7am to 9am and 4pm to 7pm on the main arteries (Mombasa Road, Thika Road, Ngong Road, and Waiyaki Way), so allow real margin for airport runs during those windows. The Nairobi Expressway is a paid alternative along the Mombasa Road corridor and can save considerable time to JKIA.

Secure parking is widely available at malls and office buildings and is worth using. Carry your driving licence, keep the vehicle documents your host provides in the car, and confirm what the insurance covers before you set off.

Common questions about car hire in Nairobi

Can I pick up a car at JKIA? Many hosts arrange handover at or near the airport. Confirm the exact meeting point with your host when you book.

Can I book same-day car hire in Nairobi? Often yes, subject to host availability. Booking ahead gives you a wider choice of vehicles and a better rate.

Do I need an international driving permit? Visitors should carry a valid national licence, and an international driving permit is recommended alongside it. Your host will confirm what they require.

Can I drive a Nairobi rental to Mombasa or Nakuru? Long-distance travel is usually fine, but agree it with your host first, as some vehicles have mileage or region conditions.

How do I pay? M-Pesa, Visa, or Mastercard through the app, with the total shown before you confirm.
