The house rules

How an ardena drive works

  1. 01

    Bring your car

    Got a car? Drive it. Every car in the convoy carries ardena merch, and we hand it out on the day.

  2. 02

    Or rent one with us

    Rent as a group and share the car, or rent on your own. Cars from KSh 3,500, with drivers at a small fee.

  3. 03

    Bring friends or make some

    Come with your crew, or ask us to pair you with others heading the same way.

  4. 04

    Join the WhatsApp group

    We share the plan, the meeting point and the timings there. Your t-shirt and snacks are waiting at the start.
