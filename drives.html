How an ardena drive works

  1. 01

    Bring your car

    Got a car? Drive it. Every car in the convoy carries ardena merch, and we hand it out on the day.

  2. 02

    Or rent one with us

    Rent as a group and share the car, or rent on your own. Cars from KSh 3,500, with drivers at a small fee.

  3. 03

    Bring friends or make some

    Come with your crew, or ask us to pair you with others heading the same way.

  4. 04

    Join the WhatsApp group

    We share the plan, the meeting point and the timings there. Your t-shirt and snacks are waiting at the start.

Questions about the trip

Anything else? WhatsApp us on +254 707 856 829 or email support@ardena.co.ke.

When is the trip?

Friday 27 to Sunday 29 November 2026, leaving from Nairobi. We share the meeting point and start time in the WhatsApp group.

What does ardena give everyone?
  • An ardena t-shirt for every person, in the size you pick in the form
  • Snacks for the road for everyone in the convoy
  • ardena merch for your car, handed out on the day
How much does it cost?

Joining is free. You cover your own fuel, plus car hire if you rent from us. Use the budget calculator above for an estimate.

I don't have a car. Can I still come?

Yes. Rent one from ardena from KSh 3,500 a day, on your own or as a group sharing the cost. Drivers are available at a small fee.

I'm coming alone. Is that okay?

Of course. Choose "pair me with others" in the form and we'll match you with a car that has a spare seat.

Can I bring friends?

Please do. Ask each friend to fill in their own form, so everyone gets a t-shirt in the right size and a place in the group.

Which way are we driving?

Out through Embu, Meru and Isiolo to Samburu, then back on the A2 through Nanyuki. About 700 km in total. See the route map.

How do I join?

Fill in the short form. We'll add you to the WhatsApp group, where we share the full plan, the meeting point and the timings.
