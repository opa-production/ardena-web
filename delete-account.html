01 / Your rights

Your right to delete.

You can delete your ardena account and the data associated with it at any time. Here’s exactly what happens when you do.

  • Your profile and account credentials are removed.
  • Your personal information, name, email, phone, is deleted from our systems.
  • Booking and transaction history linked to your account is anonymized or removed in line with legal retention requirements.
  • You will no longer be able to log in or use ardena services with that account.

Deletion is permanent. We may retain minimal data where required by law (e.g. for tax or dispute resolution).

  1. 01

    Open the app and sign in

    Use the ardena Host or ardena Renter app on the device where you’re signed in.

  2. 02

    Go to Profile or Settings

    Tap your profile icon or the menu, then open Settings.

  3. 03

    Open Account or Privacy

    Find the section that controls your account, identity, and privacy choices.

  4. 04

    Tap Delete account

    Choose “Delete account” or “Request account deletion” to start the flow.

  5. 05

    Confirm your identity

    You may be asked to re-enter your password or pass a quick identity check.

  6. 06

    Confirm and finish

    Confirm permanent deletion. You’ll get an email once your account and data have been removed.

02 / In the app

How to delete your account from the app.

The fastest way. Works for both the ardena Host and ardena Renter apps, if you’ve signed up for both, delete each account separately.

03 / Or request it here

Request deletion via this form.

If you can’t use the app, send your request through this form. We’ll verify your identity and then process the deletion of your account and associated data.

Submitting this form sends your deletion request securely to our support team. For security, avoid using shared or public computers when entering your password.
