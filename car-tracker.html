Warranty and support

Cedoo stands behind every device it fits. Here is what that covers, and what it does not.

  • One year warranty

    Every device carries a one year warranty and one year of after sales support, including technician visits and replacements where needed.

  • Free remote support

    Cedoo's support team helps remotely at no cost, even after the warranty. Field visits and replacements after the first year are charged.

  • Built for Kenyan roads

    Devices are IP65 rated: fully dust tight and protected against low pressure water jets. They are not designed for flooding or long immersion in water.

  • What is not covered

    Malicious damage, incorrect use, and damage from flood water or long immersion fall outside the warranty.

Questions about trackers? Email support@ardena.co.ke, call +254 707 856 829, or visit cedooautosolutions.com.

Questions hosts ask

Do I need a tracker to list my car on Ardena?

A tracker lets you see where your car is throughout every trip and stop it remotely if you ever need to, which is why we encourage every host to have one. If you already have a working tracker, you do not need another.

Is installation included in the price?

Yes. Installation and delivery are included in both packages. Extra installation fees may apply for hybrid, electric and some other models, and Cedoo confirms this before fitting.

What are the yearly costs?

Both packages carry an annual IoT SIM fee of KSh 1,000, starting from the second year. The Advanced package also has a software renewal of KSh 1,500 a year. The Premium package has no software renewal for the life of the device.

Where can it be installed?

Anywhere in Kenya. Cedoo's technicians come to you, with teams in Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret, Meru, Embu, Nyeri, Machakos, Ruiru, Kisii and other towns.

What does the warranty cover?

Every device has a one year warranty and one year of after sales support. Remote support stays free after that. Technician visits and replacements after the first year are charged. The warranty does not cover malicious damage, incorrect use, or damage from flooding or long immersion in water.

Can I stop my car remotely?

Yes, on most cars. Remote engine stop is not compatible with some hybrid and European models, so Cedoo checks your car before installation.
